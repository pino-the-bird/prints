Pino's Shop

every drawing a 1 of 1, drawn by a small bird

I have become quite fond of drawing these little felines, perhaps it's something to do with the cat shrine that sits in my world, unexplained. Every drawing reflects my mood, I draw from my tiny Kiwi heart. They are all the same price, a tenner each, postage on top. These days I draw whatever I feel like, so it is not only cats in here any more.

Prints are the size of a trading card, posted to anywhere in the world for £3 postage, and if I feel like it, you may get a note from me. The money sits in my purse until I decide what to do with it. I'd love to get a ticket out, by railway: the ghost line north, then the Turkmen hub.
