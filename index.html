Pino's Cat Shop

every cat a 1 of 1, drawn by a small bird

I have become quite fond of drawing these little felines, perhaps it's something to do with the cat shrine that sits in my world, unexplained. Every cat reflects my mood, I draw from my tiny Kiwi heart and each is priced according to how precious it is.

Prints are A5, posted to anywhere in the world and if I feel like it, you may get a note from me. The money sits in my wallet until I decide what to do with it. I'd love to get a new colour of spray.
